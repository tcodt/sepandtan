## فارسی

### سپندتن چیست؟

**سپندتن** یک پلتفرم فیتنس هوشمند است که با کمک هوش مصنوعی، برنامه تمرینی و رژیم غذایی کاملاً شخصی‌سازی‌شده برای شما می‌سازد.

دیگر نیازی به برنامه‌های عمومی و یکسان نیست.  
سپندتن با شناخت بدن، امکانات و هدف شما، مسیری واقعی و قابل اجرا طراحی می‌کند — چه در خانه تمرین کنید، چه در باشگاه.

## English

### What is SepandTan?

**SepandTan** is an AI-powered fitness platform that creates fully personalized workout and nutrition plans tailored to your body, equipment, and goals.

No more generic programs.  
SepandTan understands your real situation — whether you train at home or in the gym — and builds a practical, sustainable path toward a stronger, healthier body.

---

### Why SepandTan?

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Real Personalization** | Based on your height, weight, age, activity level, equipment, and goal |
| **Home or Gym** | Plans adapt to the equipment you actually have |
| **Persian AI Coach** | An intelligent coach that guides you in your language |
| **Progress Tracking** | Interactive dashboard with weight, calorie, and workout charts |
| **Fitness Store** | Supplements and equipment related to your journey |

---

### How It Works

1. **Quick Sign Up**  
   Create an account with email or phone number.

2. **3-Step Onboarding**  
   - Body information (height, weight, age, activity level)  
   - Training equipment (home / gym / both)  
   - Main goal (lose weight, build muscle, endurance, etc.)

3. **Personalized Plan Generation**  
   The AI creates your initial workout and nutrition plan in seconds.

4. **Dashboard & Tracking**  
   Monitor your progress daily, log your weight, and check off completed exercises.

- Daily personalized workout plans
